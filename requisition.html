About requisitions

  • A requisition lists the materials, equipment and services a registered practical needs (Sections C and D), for the sessions you tick.
  • Amounts are worked out for you: units × unit cost, in whole Uganda shillings.
  • It goes to your Head of Department. If it’s sent back, correct it from My practicals; it keeps its reference.
  • Once approved, print it, fill in the requisition book, have it signed by the HOD, and take it to the Finance office.
  • Questions? Email the Dean’s office or Call the Dean’s office
