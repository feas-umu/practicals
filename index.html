How it works

  1. Register each course unit’s practicals once per semester. It goes straight into the department’s register; no approval is needed.
  2. Need materials, equipment or services? Make a requisition for the sessions that need them, now or later from My practicals.
  3. Your Head of Department approves the requisition by email, or sends it back with a comment.
  4. Print the approved requisition, fill in the requisition book, have it signed by the HOD, and take it to the Finance office.

Good to know

  • Use your university email. The first time on a new phone or computer we email you a 6-digit code.
  • One registration per course unit per semester. To change it, open it from My practicals → Edit practical.
  • A practical can have several requisitions, e.g. one for the lab sessions and one for a field trip.
  • Water Engineering requisitions go to the Head of Civil Engineering.
  • Questions? Email the Dean’s office or Call the Dean’s office
